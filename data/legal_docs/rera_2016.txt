PART I — PRELIMINARY
=============================================================================

SECTION 2 — DEFINITIONS

Clause 2(b) — "apartment" means any building or part of a building, including
apartment, flat, unit, or any other name, containing a separate unit of a real
estate project and includes any covered garage, whether or not adjacent to the
building in which such apartment is located.

Clause 2(c) — "carpet area" means the net usable floor area of an apartment,
excluding the area covered by the external walls, areas under service shafts,
exclusive balcony or verandah area and exclusive open terrace area, but includes
the area covered by the internal partition walls of the apartment.
NOTE: For the first time under law, 'carpet area' replaces 'super built-up area'
as the mandated unit of measurement for sale transactions.

Clause 2(k) — "estimated cost of real estate project" means the total cost
involved in developing the real estate project and includes the land cost,
taxes, fees, charges and all other expenses for completing the project.

Clause 2(zn) — "promoter" means a person who constructs or causes to be
constructed an independent building or a building consisting of apartments, or
converts an existing building or part thereof into apartments, for the purpose
of selling all or some of the apartments to other persons and includes a
developer, builder, housing board, apex co-operative housing finance society,
or a State level housing cooperative society and real estate agent.

Clause 2(zr) — "real estate agent" means any person who negotiates or acts on
behalf of one person in a transaction of transfer of his plot, apartment or
building, as the case may be, in a real estate project, by way of sale, with
another person or transfer of plot, apartment or building, by way of lease,
to another person and receives remuneration or fees or any other charges for
his services, whether as commission or otherwise.

PART II — REGISTRATION OF REAL ESTATE PROJECT AND REAL ESTATE AGENTS
=============================================================================

SECTION 3 — PRIOR REGISTRATION OF REAL ESTATE PROJECT WITH REAL ESTATE
REGULATORY AUTHORITY

Clause 3(1): No promoter shall advertise, market, book, sell or offer for sale,
or invite persons to purchase in any manner any plot, apartment or building, as
the case may be, in any real estate project or part of it, in any planning area,
without registering the real estate project with the Real Estate Regulatory
Authority established under this Act.
PENALTY: Any promoter who fails to register the project is liable to pay a
penalty up to 10% of the estimated project cost under Section 59.

Clause 3(2): Projects where the area of land proposed to be developed does not
exceed 500 square metres or the number of apartments proposed to be developed
does not exceed 8 inclusive of all phases are EXEMPT from mandatory RERA
registration. Individual residential properties and buildings without sale
of units are also exempt.

Clause 3(3): Where the real estate project is to be developed in phases, every
such phase shall be considered a standalone real estate project and the promoter
shall obtain registration under this Act for each phase separately.

SECTION 4 — APPLICATION FOR REGISTRATION OF REAL ESTATE PROJECTS

Clause 4(1): Every promoter shall make an application to the Authority for
registration of the real estate project in such form, manner, within such time
and accompanied by such fee as may be specified by regulations made by the
Authority.

Clause 4(2)(l): The promoter shall declare in the application that 70% of the
amounts realised for the real estate project from the allottees, from time to
time, shall be deposited in a separate account to be maintained in a scheduled
bank to cover the cost of construction and the land cost and shall be used only
for that purpose.
CRITICAL BUYER PROTECTION: The 70% escrow account rule is designed to prevent
fund diversion and is one of RERA's most significant consumer protections.

Clause 4(4): The registration granted under this section shall be valid for a
period declared by the promoter for completion of the real estate project. The
Authority may extend the period of registration if the project is delayed due
to force majeure circumstances.

SECTION 5 — GRANT OF REGISTRATION

Clause 5(1): On receipt of the application, if the Authority is satisfied that
the documents are in order, it shall register the real estate project and grant
a registration number, including a login ID and password for accessing the
website of the Authority within 30 days.

SECTION 6 — EXTENSION OF REGISTRATION

Clause 6: The registration granted under Section 5 may be extended by the
Authority on an application made by the promoter due to force majeure, which
shall, on the recommendation of the Authority, include war, flood, drought,
fire, cyclone, earthquake or any other calamity caused by nature.

SECTION 7 — REVOCATION OF REGISTRATION

Clause 7(1): The Authority may revoke the registration granted under Section 5
if the promoter:
(a) makes wilful default in doing anything required of him by or under this Act
    or the rules or regulations made thereunder; or
(b) has violated any of the terms or conditions of the approval given by the
    competent authority; or
(c) is involved in any kind of unfair practice or irregularities.

PART III — FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES OF PROMOTERS
=============================================================================

SECTION 11 — FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES OF PROMOTER

Clause 11(4)(a): The promoter shall not accept a sum more than 10% of the cost
of the apartment, plot or building as the case may be, as an advance payment or
an application fee, from a person without first entering into a written
agreement for sale with such person.

Clause 11(4)(b): The promoter shall not make any additions or alterations in the
sanctioned plans, layout plans and specifications and the nature of fixtures,
fittings and amenities in respect of the apartment, plot or building, as
approved by the competent authorities, other than in the manner provided under
the Act or the rules and regulations made thereunder.

SECTION 12 — OBLIGATIONS OF PROMOTER REGARDING VERACITY OF
ADVERTISEMENT

Clause 12: Where any person makes an advance or a deposit on the basis of the
information contained in the notice, advertisement or prospectus, or on the
basis of any model apartment, plot or building, and sustains any loss or damage
by reason of any incorrect, false statement included therein, he shall be
compensated by the promoter in the manner as provided under this Act.

SECTION 13 — NO DEPOSIT OR ADVANCE TO BE TAKEN WITHOUT AGREEMENT FOR SALE

Clause 13(1): A promoter shall not accept a sum more than ten per cent of the
cost of the apartment, plot or building as the case may be, as an advance
payment or an application fee from a person without first entering into a written
agreement for sale with such person and register the said agreement for sale.

SECTION 14 — ADHERENCE TO SANCTIONED PLANS AND PROJECT SPECIFICATIONS
BY THE PROMOTER

Clause 14(1): The proposed project shall be developed and completed by the
promoter in accordance with the sanctioned plans, layout plans and specifications
as approved by the competent authorities.

Clause 14(3): In case any structural defect or any other defect in workmanship,
quality or provision of services or any other obligations of the promoter as per
the agreement for sale relating to such development is brought to the notice of
the promoter within 5 years by the allottee from the date of handing over
possession, it shall be the duty of the promoter to rectify such defects
without further charge, within 30 days.

SECTION 17 — TRANSFER OF TITLE

Clause 17: The promoter shall execute a registered conveyance deed in favour of
the allottee along with the undivided proportionate title in the common areas to
the association of allottees or the competent authority, as the case may be, in
a real estate project, and hand over the physical possession of the plot,
apartment of building, as the case may be, to the allottees and the common
areas to the association of allottees or the competent authority, as the case
may be.

SECTION 18 — RETURN OF AMOUNT AND COMPENSATION

Clause 18(1): If the promoter fails to complete or is unable to give possession
of an apartment, plot or building, as the case may be, in accordance with the
terms of the agreement for sale or due to discontinuance of his business as a
developer on account of suspension or revocation of the registration under this
Act, or for any other reason, he shall be liable, on demand being made by the
allottee, to return the amount received by him in respect of that apartment,
plot, building along with interest at such rate as may be prescribed and
compensation in the manner as provided under this Act.

SECTION 19 — RIGHTS AND DUTIES OF ALLOTTEES

Clause 19(1): Every allottee shall be entitled to obtain the information relating
to sanctioned plans, layout plans and specifications approved by the competent
authority and such other information as provided in this Act or the rules and
regulations made thereunder.

Clause 19(4): Every allottee shall have the right to claim the possession of
apartment, plot or building as the case may be, and the association of allottees
shall have the right to claim the possession of common areas, as per the
declaration given by the promoter under sub-clause (C) of clause (l) of
sub-section (2) of section 4.

Clause 19(6): Every allottee shall be responsible to take possession of
apartment, plot or building as the case may be, from the promoter after issuance
of the Occupancy Certificate by the competent authority.

PART V — REAL ESTATE REGULATORY AUTHORITY — APPELLATE TRIBUNAL
=============================================================================

SECTION 31 — COMPLAINTS

Clause 31(1): Any aggrieved person may file a complaint with the Authority or
the adjudicating officer, as the case may be, for any violation or contravention
of the provisions of this Act or the rules and regulations made thereunder.

SECTION 38 — POWERS OF AUTHORITY

Clause 38(1): The Authority shall have powers to impose penalty or interest, in
regard to any contravention of obligations cast upon the promoters, the
allottees and the real estate agents under this Act or the rules and regulations
made thereunder.

=============================================================================
PART VI — PENALTIES
=============================================================================

SECTION 59 — PENALTY FOR NON-REGISTRATION

Clause 59(1): If any promoter contravenes the provisions of Section 3, he shall
be liable to a penalty which may extend up to 10% of the estimated cost of the
real estate project.

Clause 59(2): If any promoter does not comply with the orders, decisions or
directions issued under sub-section (1) or continues to violate the provisions
of Section 3, he shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may
extend up to 3 years or with fine which may extend up to a further 10% of the
estimated cost of the real estate project, or with both.

SECTION 60 — PENALTY FOR WRONGFUL DISCLOSURE

Clause 60: If any promoter contravenes any other provisions of this Act, other
than that provided under Section 3 or Section 4, he shall be liable to a penalty
which may extend up to 5% of the estimated cost of the real estate project.

SECTION 63 — PENALTY FOR REAL ESTATE AGENTS

Clause 63: If any real estate agent fails to comply with or contravenes any of
the provisions of Section 9 or Section 10, he shall be liable to a penalty of
10,000 rupees for each day during which such default continues, which may
cumulatively extend up to 5% of the cost of plot, apartment or buildings, as
the case may be, of the real estate project for which the sale or purchase has
been facilitated.

SECTION 64 — PENALTY ON ALLOTTEES

Clause 64: If any allottee, who fails to comply with, or contravenes any of
the provisions of this Act or the rules or regulations made thereunder, he shall
be liable to the penalty as may be determined by the adjudicating officer.
